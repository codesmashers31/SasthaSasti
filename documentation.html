1. Executive Summary & Solution Architecture

Sastha Sasti / Divya Murti ERP is a specialized enterprise resource planning and point-of-sale solution custom-engineered for god statue manufacturers, sthapathi sculptors, bronze and brass foundries, religious artifact showrooms, and devotional product wholesalers.

Traditional generic retail POS software lacks support for the dimensional attributes, metal compositions (Panchaloha, Brass, Black Granite, Marble, Teak), piece-rate artisan labor markups, and sacred idol taxonomy required by religious artifact enterprises. This platform closes that gap with a high-throughput, sub-second POS terminal, multi-mode payment collection, automated GST compliance (HSN 9701 / 12% GST), an immutable double-entry stock ledger, and deep supplier artisan accounting.

Architectural Highlights
  • Pure Core MVC: Lightweight, ultra-fast routing engine without bulky third-party framework overhead.
  • Prepared Statements: 100% parameter-bound SQL queries preventing SQL injection vulnerabilities.
  • Session & CSRF Security: Automatic cryptographic tokens validating every mutating POST/AJAX transaction.
  • Client-Side Resilience: Held carts persisted in local browser storage across hardware restarts.
Business Capabilities
  • Instant A4 / Thermal Billing: Print GST tax invoices or 80mm/58mm thermal receipts instantly.
  • Artisan Foundries P&L: Track piece-rate purchase costs, markups, and supplier debt in real time.
  • Granular Role Security: Independent interfaces tailored for Super Admins down to dedicated POS Cashiers.
  • Forensic Audit Trail: Every critical login, update, sale, and stock adjustment is logged with IP and timestamp.

The application enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Access to every route and controller action is guarded by middleware checking against the `permissions` and `role_permissions` database tables. Additionally, custom overrides can be assigned to individual users via `user_permissions`.

5. Core Application Modules & Feature Inventory

5.1 Executive Dashboard

The executive dashboard aggregates real-time business health metrics across the enterprise:

  • Financial KPI Cards: Displays Total Sales Today, Month-to-date Revenue, Total Invoices Generated, Paid Amount Collected, and Current Outstanding Customer Dues.
  • Inventory Critical Alerts: Live monitoring of idols reaching reorder thresholds with immediate "Reorder" action triggers.
  • Dynamic Trend Charts: Integrated Chart.js visualizations demonstrating daily and weekly sales trajectory.
  • Deity Popularity Index: Ranks highest-velocity statues (e.g., 12-inch Brass Ganesha, Panchaloha Nataraja) by sales volume.

5.2 Sacred POS Billing Terminal (/billing, /pos)

Engineered for rapid counter operations during high-traffic festival seasons and temple peak hours:

  • Lightning Search & Quick Categories: Instant sub-300ms fuzzy search by deity name, SKU, material, or barcode with quick category filter tabs.
  • Barcode Scanner Integration: Automatically intercepts USB/Bluetooth barcode scanner inputs; typing or scanning barcodes automatically pulls up items with stock validation.
  • Cart Management & Dynamic Modifiers: Real-time quantity steppers, item-level discounts, stock quantity threshold enforcement, and removal triggers.
  • Bill-Level GST Customization: Billing operators can select custom GST rates (e.g., 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%) or apply item defaults, dynamically updating taxable breakdowns.
  • Multi-Channel Payment Engine: Supports Cash, UPI (with QR code simulation), Credit/Debit Cards, Bank Direct Transfer, and Customer Ledger Credit. Also supports split-mode settlements.
  • Held Carts (Suspend & Resume): Allows cashiers to suspend multiple active carts to attend to other customers, saving them safely in the browser's localStorage.
  • Instant Dual-Format Print Triggers: Instant generation and printing of:
    • A4 GST Tax Invoices: Formal tax document complete with company GSTIN, HSN codes, CGST/SGST decomposition, customer address, and authorized signature section.
    • Thermal Receipts (80mm & 58mm): Compact receipt format designed for rapid thermal receipt printers with company header, item list, and barcode.

5.3 Customer Invoices Register (/customer-invoices)

A unified register for reviewing, filtering, and managing completed and pending sales bills:

  • Advanced Multi-Criteria Filtering: Filter by invoice number, customer phone number, payment status (Paid, Partial, Unpaid), invoice status, and custom date spans.
  • Key Financial Metrics: Immediate top-level display of Total Invoiced Revenue, Net Collected Cash, and Total Outstanding Credit Receivables.
  • Actions & Operations: View interactive invoice details modal, reprint A4 invoices, reprint thermal slips, export to PDF, cancel invoices with audit notes, and accept due payments.

5.4 Product Management & Statue Catalog (/products)

The product catalog features an interactive four-pillar tabbed interface designed for seamless idol management:

1. Supplier Details Tab

Manages artisan sculptors and foundries. Provides a real-time table of all registered suppliers, contact information, product count, payment status, and an inline modal to register suppliers along with initial product associations.

2. Product Details Tab

Full catalog entries for statues. Captures specific attributes: Deity Name, Material, Dimensions (Height, Width, Depth, Weight), Finish Type (Antique, Gold Plated, Polished), Piece Rate, Markup %, Selling Price, Wholesale Price, and Low-stock limits.

3. Barcode Details Tab

Automated barcode label generation. Configures SKU tracking parameters, generates scannable Code128 barcodes, and provides a multi-label print preview ready for thermal sticker printers.

4. Invoice Details Tab

Tracks supplier purchase transactions, invoice numbers, total purchase value, paid amount, and payment status (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid), with instant payment update modals.

  • Bulk Data Exchange: Features one-click CSV/Excel Catalog Export, Bulk CSV Import with validation, and a downloadable standard CSV template for fast inventory initialization.

5.5 Categories & Subcategories (/categories, /subcategories)

  • Hierarchical Deities Taxonomy: Organizes inventory into primary categories (e.g., Brass Statues, Marble Murtis, Wooden Artifacts) and deity subcategories (Lord Ganesha, Lord Shiva, Goddess Lakshmi, Lord Murugan, Perumal, Temple Bells).
  • Category Management: Unique category codes, descriptions, cover image upload, and bulk CSV Import/Export.

5.6 Stock Control & Inventory Transaction Ledger (/inventory)

  • Immutable Double-Entry Ledger: Every single piece mutation is strictly recorded in inventory_transactions with reference type, previous stock, quantity change, new stock, and operator user ID.
  • Transaction Classification: Distinguishes between stock_in, stock_out, sale, purchase, sales_return, adjustment, damage, and transfer.
  • Stock Adjustment Modal: Allows authorized staff to perform physical audit counts and register adjustments with mandatory reason documentation.

5.7 Artisans, Foundries & Supplier Payments (/suppliers, /billing/invoices)

  • Artisan Profiles: Maintains master records of master sculptors (Sthapathis), bronze foundries, and woodcarvers, including banking IFSC codes, account numbers, and GST credentials.
  • Supplier Payment Tracking: Integrated tracking through supplier_payments and supplier_payment_history tables.
  • Payment Modal & History: Enables accountants to record partial or full payments, choose payment mode (Cash, Bank Transfer, UPI, Cheque), record transaction reference IDs, and view chronological disbursement histories.

5.8 Purchase Procurement Orders (/purchases)

  • Procurement Lifecycle: Supports multi-tier purchase workflow: draft → pending → approved → received.
  • Automatic Stock Inward: Approving or marking a purchase as received automatically increments inventory in the catalog and creates corresponding ledger entries in inventory_transactions.

5.9 Customer CRM & Ledgers (/customers)

  • Customer Categorization: Profiles categorized into Retail Walk-in, Wholesale Distributors, and Temple Trusts / Devasthanams.
  • Credit & Ledger Control: Enforces maximum credit limits and tracks running outstanding ledger balances across billing cycles.

5.10 Accounts, Cash Book & Bank Registers (/accounts)

  • Chart of Accounts: Dual registers managing Physical Counter Cash and Bank Current Accounts (e.g., HDFC, SBI).
  • Receipts & Disbursements: Centralized recording of customer payment collections against unpaid invoices and supplier payouts.
  • Running Balance Tracking: Real-time ledger entries in account_transactions with credit/debit validation.

5.11 Expense Vouchers (/expenses)

  • Voucher Generation: Creation of expense vouchers across categories: Showroom Rent, Freight & Logistics, Electricity, Packaging, Wages, and Workshop Advances.
  • Payment Reconciliation: Automatic debiting from the specified Cash or Bank account upon voucher approval.

5.12 Sales Returns & Restocking (/sales-returns)

  • Invoice Lookup Engine: Cashiers can search the original invoice number to automatically populate purchased items and prices.
  • Restocking & Refunds: Returning items immediately restocks them back into warehouse inventory and generates a cash/UPI/credit note refund voucher.

5.13 Analytics, Valuation & P&L Reports (/reports)

  • Sales Analytics Report: Visual breakdown of daily, weekly, and monthly sales trends, gross revenue, discount totals, and tax collections.
  • Stock Valuation Report: Analyzes real-time warehouse inventory value at cost price versus retail selling price, highlighting dead stock and fast-moving idols.
  • Financial P&L Statement: Real-time Profit & Loss statement summarizing Sales Income, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Operating Expenses, and Net Operating Margins.

5.14 System Settings & Forensic Activity Audit (/settings, /audit)

  • Company Branding: Configures business legal name, branch address, contact numbers, GSTIN, currency symbol (₹), and invoice number prefixes.
  • Forensic Activity Trail: Immutable log table recording user ID, action type, module, record ID, IP address, user agent, and timestamp for complete internal compliance.

7. Operational Workflows & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

7.1 Daily Counter POS Billing Workflow

  1. Cashier Sign-in: Log in using assigned POS cashier credentials (e.g., priya / admin123). The interface opens directly to the Sacred POS Billing Terminal.
  2. Item Addition: Point the barcode scanner at idol labels or search by deity name / size. Items instantly populate the active cart with default selling price and stock verification.
  3. Customer Identification: For retail walk-ins, leave as Counter Customer. For regular temple patrons or wholesalers, enter their mobile number to auto-populate or create their profile.
  4. Tax & Discount Selection: Adjust item discount or overall bill discount if authorized. Select custom GST tax % or retain standard 12% GST.
  5. Payment & Checkout: Click checkout, select tender mode (Cash, UPI, Card, Bank Transfer, Ledger Credit), enter amount paid, and confirm transaction.
  6. Print Invoicing: The print dialog triggers automatically. Choose A4 GST Tax Invoice for institutional orders or Thermal Receipt for retail counter patrons.

7.2 Artisan Sourcing & Supplier Settlement Workflow

  1. New Artisan Registration: Open Artisans & Foundries or the Supplier Details Tab in Product Management. Record sculptor name, phone number, foundry location, bank IFSC, and account number.
  2. Product Association & Costing: Record received idols with piece-rate labor cost, markup percentage, and calculated selling price.
  3. Payment Disbursement: In Supplier Purchase & Payment Management (/billing/invoices), click Update Payment for the supplier. Record the disbursed amount, payment method (Bank/UPI/Cash), date, and reference UTR.
  4. Audit Verification: The system immediately updates the supplier's balance, status (Paid / Partial / Unpaid), and records an immutable log in supplier_payment_history.

7.3 Day-End Cash Balancing & Financial Reconciliation

  1. Sales Verification: Access Customer Invoice Details (/customer-invoices) and filter by today's date to verify total invoiced volume and collected cash.
  2. Cash Register Balancing: Open Accounts & Cash Book (/accounts). Match physical drawer cash against the calculated running balance in the Cash register.
  3. Expense Reconciliation: Verify that all petty cash payments made during the day have corresponding approved vouchers in Expense Vouchers (/expenses).
  4. Activity Audit Review: Store Admin or Super Admin reviews the Audit Trail (/audit) to verify any invoice cancellations or stock adjustments performed during the business day.
Database Backup Routine

It is strongly recommended to run daily database backups. An initial reference backup is maintained at god_statue_erp_backup.sql. To create a fresh daily snapshot via command line:

mysqldump -u root god_statue_erp > "C:\xampp\htdocs\billing\billingsoftwaregod\backup_$(Get-Date -Format 'yyyyMMdd').sql"
